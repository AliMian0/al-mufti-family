Late Mughal Era

Jalal-ud-din — “Mufti Sahib”

An esteemed Islamic jurist and scholar whose interpretations and rulings were sought after and relied upon by the rulers of the time. His name became the family's own.

Generations Since

The family in Muftian & Dina
